2. Sheet & cut settings

Kerf: enter the measured blade width. If left blank, the layout is provisional and assumes 0 mm kerf. Quick, Standard and Deep use diversified deterministic ordering and placement strategies. Deep also probes whether the final board can be eliminated. The seed reproduces a search. All accepted layouts are checked against a hierarchical guillotine cut tree. Edge banding can be detected from _ / ~ markers or edited per row. The cutting-list table displays saved 0.8 mm and 2 mm counts together. L means the length dimension and W means the width dimension. Each count is the number of edges on that dimension (0–2).
