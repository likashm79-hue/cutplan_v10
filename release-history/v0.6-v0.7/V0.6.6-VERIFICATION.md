# CutPlan v0.6.6 — Guided Edge Banding Verification

**Date:** 10 November 2026  
**Release:** CutPlan v0.6.6  
**Scope:** Sequential edge-banding entry only. No optimizer, parser, nesting, drawing, cut-tree or PDF source was changed.

## User workflow

1. Open the full-screen Edge Banding workspace.
2. The first cutting-list row appears with its reference, length, width, quantity and `Part 1 of N` progress.
3. Enter the counts for 0.8 mm length edges, 0.8 mm width edges, 2 mm length edges and 2 mm width edges.
4. Values save as they are typed to that exact row.
5. Next saves and advances. Previous returns to the prior row and restores its values.
6. The final part displays Finish; Finish saves and closes the dialog.
7. Reopening the editor resumes at the last-edited row. Escape and Close also dismiss the dialog.

- Used Next and Previous and verified that values were restored for each reference.
- Entered an invalid count of 3 and confirmed navigation was blocked on the current part.
- Used Finish and verified the dialog closed.
- Reopened the editor and confirmed it resumed at the last edited reference.
- Verified the calculated material totals from part dimensions, counts and quantities.
- Verified Escape closes the dialog and that the document/table do not overflow horizontally at the narrow viewport.

## Scope and caveats

The existing net linear-length calculation remains unchanged. This release does not add purchase allowance, stock-roll rounding, end-trimming allowance or legacy CSV material-reconciliation logic. It also does not add new restrictions across the combined 0.8 mm and 2 mm edge counts. Those are separate calculation/business-rule decisions, not part of the sequential-entry UI change.
